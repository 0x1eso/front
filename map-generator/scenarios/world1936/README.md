# World War 1936 map sources

See docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-world-war-1936-design.md §4. From map-generator/:

1. `bash scenarios/world1936/fetch.sh` — Natural Earth and NASA files into `.cache/ww1936` (not committed): the Natural Earth layers (rivers too), the GEBCO topography, and the MODIS IGBP land cover (2024, a 0.1° grid through NASA GIBS, with its colour map; NASA NEO, which the P2 plan named, closed in September 2026). A file whose checksum does not match fails the script.
2. `go run ./cmd/ww1936build -bootstrap-cities` — only when the city lists in `cmd/ww1936build/cities.go` change; writes `src/vp_cities.json`.
3. `go run ./cmd/ww1936build` — writes `assets/maps/world1936/image.png`, `regions.png` and `scenario.src.json` here, and prints the land graph (edges, straits, big rivers, terrain per kind), the sea zones it cut and their graph, the islands and the land tiles. `-debug-png .cache/ww1936/owners.png` also draws the owners for checking by eye.
4. `go run -tags nodynamic . --maps=world1936` — the map files in `resources/maps/world1936`. This step also caps each capital region at a third of its country's points (`capCapitalVP`, spec §7.5), so `scenario.json` can hold less than `scenario.src.json` there.

The committed files were made on linux/amd64 with Go 1.27.2. Steps 2–4 are deterministic there: running them again gives the same bytes. Other architectures can round floating point differently (fused multiply-add on arm64, for example) and move a pixel here and there, so regenerate on linux/amd64 with that Go version, run Prettier on `scenario.src.json` and `src/vp_cities.json` (the generator formats its own JSON), and byte-compare: `git status` must show no change.

`src/economy1936.json` (P1 spec §3.2) gives each of the 79 countries its 1936 population (thousands, homeland and colonies), industrialisation `dev` 1–5, and for the majors (and Czechoslovakia's and Sweden's mil) its factories and infrastructure; `nonCore` lists the modern countries of its land that are not homeland. `src/industry1936.json` lists the 1936 industrial centres (a multiplier on the factory weight of the region they fall in) and naval yards (where the dockyards go first); the USA's row in `economy1936.json` also carries the 1930 census per state (`admPop`). `src/resources1936.json` (spec §3.5) lists the resource sites (owner tag, resource, daily amount, lon/lat). Step 3 repeats the generator's islet and pond cleanup to know the final coasts and the regions that will be dropped (they get nothing), and turns the tables into each region's population (each country's spread by the Natural Earth populated places in its regions, each place counted at most 3 million, plus a little per area), category, infrastructure, homeland, coast, factories and resources (`cmd/ww1936build/economy.go`, spec §3.3) and prints the factory totals per country; both files are historical figures, rounded.

`scenario.src.json` and `scenario.json` are version 5 (P6 spec §16.1, below; version 4 is P4 spec §3.6, version 3 P2 spec §3.1, version 2 P1 spec §3.1). Step 4 copies the economy fields, recomputes `coastal` on the final tiles, moves the population and resources of a region the generator drops (an islet under 30 tiles) to its country's capital region, and fails on a dropped region with factories or a dockyard off the coast.

`src/owners1936.json` gives every modern country (Natural Earth `adm0_a3`) its 1936 owner, overrides admin-1 units (`"ADM0:name"`), and patches the borders that cut through modern units with hand-drawn polygons (each patch limited to the listed modern countries or units). Its `carve` rows cut admin-1 units out of the finished regions as a region of their own (`regions.go carveUnits`, after the islands): the other regions keep the shapes they had without them, and a region of another modern country keeps the units' pixels. Nagasaki is one (P6 plan Task 15, controller ruling of 2026-10-21, for the atomic targets): alone the prefecture is 16 tiles in three pieces on this map, which the generator's 20-tile fragment rule would give back to Kumamoto, so the row takes Nagasaki and Saga (old Hizen) together: 50 tiles, id 1369. `src/region_names.json` renames a generated region (`"TAG:name": [name, nameKo]`).

Cross-check (optional): with aourednik/historical-basemaps' `world_1938.geojson` (GPL-3.0) in `.cache/ww1936`, `go run ./cmd/ww1936build -crosscheck .cache/ww1936/world_1938.geojson` prints how much of each country lies in its best match. It writes no file; nothing derived from that data is committed.

## Version 3: the land graph and the P4 map fixes

P2 spec §3 and P4 spec §3.1–3.3 (P2 plan Task 2), all in step 3:

- **Region ids stay.** The tool reads the committed `resources/maps/world1936/scenario.json` (`-ids`) and gives each region the id its owner and name have there (`land.go stableIDs`); new islands take the next ids in `src/islands1936.json` order (1343..1366), then other new regions (Hatay, 1367), then the regions the generator drops. An id of the file without a region fails the build.
- **Islands** (`src/islands1936.json`, `islands.go`): the 24 islands of P4 spec §3.2 are regions of their own: their Natural Earth land pixels, or `stampTiles` pixels of water made land around the point when the rasteriser gave none (Midway, Wake, Kwajalein, Iwo Jima, Tarawa, Heligoland). `scenario.src.json` lists their points (`keepIslands`): the generator keeps every land body within 3 tiles of one (`map_generator.go removeSmallIslands`), and the 20-tile fragment rule leaves island regions alone. `port`, `airBase`, `navalBase`, `coastalFort` are the P4 start values; only `port` is read now. Hatay (from Aleppo) is an Own patch of `src/owners1936.json`.
- **Sea zones** (`src/seasplit1936.json`, `seasplit.go`): the eleven boxes of P4 spec §3.1 cut 40150..40160 out of their zones (the zones are drawn on the land before the islands are stamped, so their numbering stays); a piece a cut leaves apart joins the zone it shares the longest edge with. 40062 becomes the Central Mediterranean; the Caspian, Lake Superior and Lake Michigan are lakes.
- **The final map**: the tool repeats the generator's cleanup (`land.go finalMap`, `generatorLand`) and computes everything on it; the generator checks every border against its own tiles and fails on a difference.
- **Land edges** (`land.go`): 4-neighbour tile pairs between regions; km between the two centres (the tile nearest each region's centroid) on the globe; a river edge when 40% of the border pairs lie within 2 tiles of a Natural Earth river (big when the river's scalerank is 4 or less, and the Vistula and the Don, which P2 spec §3.2-3 names); a strait edge between regions on two landmasses whose land lies within 6 water tiles, plus the hand pairs of `src/straits1936.json` `land.add` (Kerch, Gibraltar: one landmass) less `land.remove` (crossings HOI4 has none of: Rhodes and Leros to Turkey, Malaya and Singapore to Sumatra, Kamchatka to the Kurils, Scotland to Ulster, Papua to the Solomons, Lolland and Falster to Germany).
- **Terrain**: the MODIS class most of a region's tiles fall in (forest 1–5, jungle for evergreen broadleaf in the tropics, marsh 11, desert 16), else the v1 class; urban for a region under 400 tiles with a city of tier 10 or more; then the hand table `src/terrain1936.json` (the Pripyat marshes, drained after 1936 so MODIS reads farmland, and the Ob swamps).
- **Rail and forts**: rail from the infrastructure (P2 spec §3.2-6) raised to the axes of `src/rail1936.json`; forts from `src/forts1936.json` (P2 spec §3.2-7). Both name regions as `"TAG:name"`; an unknown name fails.
- **Sea edges** (`seaedges.go`): zones sharing 3 water tile pairs or more; the straits and canals of `src/straits1936.json` mark that edge or add one; zones meeting in 3 rows or more across the date line (the map's two end columns). Lakes have none.
- The generator copies all of it to `scenario.json`, drops what a dropped region held, and sets `port` on the final coast (coastal and a dockyard, 5 points or a hand harbour).

## Version 4: strategic regions, harbours, start bases, the row table

P4 spec §3.4–3.6, §6.4, §6.5, §12.1 and P4 plan decisions 6, 8 and 9 (P4 plan Task 2), in step 3 (`cmd/ww1936build/strategic.go`, `bases.go`, `rows.go`) and step 4 (`map-generator/regions.go buildV4`). `map.bin`, `regions.bin` and the region and zone ids do not change: a strategic region is a group of regions or zones.

- **Sea strategic regions** (`src/seaRegions1936.json`, the table of spec §3.4: id 1001.., names, zones less 40000): every zone that is not a lake in exactly one, or the build fails. A zone's `center` is its water tile nearest its centroid (ties: the smaller tile), `latC` the Miller latitude of that row ×100, `wcell` the P2 weather cell, `sr` its region (0 for a lake). A region's centre is the member zone centre nearest the centroid of all its tiles.
- **Land strategic regions** (`src/landRegions1936.json`: the hand seeds, `keepAlone` for Malta, Pantelleria and Rhodes): a landmass is the regions joined by land edges that are not straits. A hand seed is the region at its point (the nearest land tile's when the point is water; two seeds in one region fail). A landmass without a seed, of at most 33 regions and 200,000 tiles, is one strategic region; in any other, a multi-source Dijkstra (edge km) gives every region its nearest seed (ties: the earlier seed, hand seeds in file order, then auto seeds as made), and while a share holds more than 33 regions, or more than 200,000 tiles in more than one region, the region farthest from every seed (ties: the smaller id; the smallest when there is no seed) becomes a seed. A seedless strategic region of at most 3 regions and one owner joins the nearest strategic region (not a `keepAlone` one) holding a region of that owner whose centre lies within 600 km of its centre (ties: the smaller first region); hand-seeded regions never join another. Names: the hand seed's, an auto seed region's, or a seedless landmass's smallest region's. Ids 1.. by smallest region; the centre is the member region centre nearest the centroid of all its tiles. The tool prints the thresholds, every seed's region, every land strategic region and every island that joined one, and fails outside 150..185.
  - The seeds: spec §3.4-2's list (Europe, North Africa, the Middle East, East Asia), with Sumatra and Java as one seed (one region on this map, Batavia); the HOI4-like regions outside Europe of the controller ruling of 2026-10-21 (India, Persia, Arabia, Afghanistan, Soviet Asia, China and Mongolia, Siam and Indochina, Africa, the Americas, Australia); the Korean Peninsula (exactly its eleven regions) and Taiwan as neutral geographic zones (ruling of the same day); and names for islands that are strategic regions of their own anyway (Iceland, Greenland, Madagascar, the Arctic archipelagos…). 151 seeds.
  - The thresholds: the spec's 12 regions / 40,000 tiles made 479 land strategic regions with the spec's seeds. The rulings raised the target from 110..140 to 150..185; the 151 hand-seeded regions and the 30 islands no region of their owner lies near make 181, and 33 regions / 200,000 tiles (`srMaxRegions`, `srMaxTiles`; `-sr-regions`, `-sr-tiles`) is the finest split without an auto seed (185,000 tiles adds one: 182; 32 regions makes 193).
- **Harbours** (spec §3.5): a region's harbour is the zone (lakes left out) its tiles touch along the most 4-neighbour pairs (ties: the smaller id), 0 for a region on no sea (the Caspian and Great Lakes shores are coastal with harbour 0). Step 4 recomputes them on the final tiles and fails on a difference.
- **Start levels**: `src/navalBases1936.json` (spec §6.4) and `src/coastalForts1936.json` (spec §12.1) name places by lon/lat and their 1936 owner (`tag`, checked): the coastal region (one with a harbour) at the point, or the one of the nearest such tile within 30 tiles when the point is water or an inland region; two places in one region keep the larger level. Chinhae and Kaohsiung are left out (Korea and Taiwan take the formula). The islands of `src/islands1936.json` add their `navalBase` (0 included), `airBase` and `coastalFort` when above 0. Step 4 applies the formulas where no hand level is given: a naval base `min(3, 1 + dock)` in a P2 port with a harbour; the air base of spec §12.1 (a major's capital 5, a major's region of 10 points or more 3, of 5 or more 1, another country's capital 3 at dev 3 or more else 1, its regions of 10 points or more 1), the anti-air (a major's capital 3, a major's region of 10 points or more 1, another country's capital at dev 3 or more 1), no coastal fort. `scenario.src.json` carries the majors for it.
- **Row table** (plan decision 9): `rows.latC` (each row's centre latitude ×100) and `rows.kmxMilli` (one tile's east-west great-circle km ×1000 there, `40075 × cos(lat) / width`), for engine-lib `ScenarioIndex.kmBetween`; `rows_test.go` checks the formula against the haversine on twenty pairs of places (within 1%).
- **1936 dockyards** (spec §6.5): `src/economy1936.json` gives Germany 8, Italy 9, Japan 14, the UK 20, France 10, the USA 16, the USSR 8, the Netherlands 3, Sweden 2, both Spains 2 each, Canada and Australia 2 each; they are placed by the P1 rule (naval yards first, then coastal regions by weight, a category up when no slot is free). The tool prints the dockyards per country.

## Version 5: coal, uranium, hydro, landmarks and dams

P6 spec §16.1–§16.4 (P6 plan Task 15), in step 3 (`cmd/ww1936build/energy.go`) and step 4 (`map-generator/regions.go buildV5`). Region and zone ids do not change but Nagasaki's (above).

- `src/energy1936.json` lists the coal fields, uranium mines and hydro power as sites (owner tag, amount, lon/lat, note): each lands in the region of the nearest land tile within 8 tiles on the final map, which must be its tag's, and a region's value is the sum of its sites (coal at most 8, uranium and hydro at most 6). Its `sources` say where the figures come from: the coal is the 1936 output of the U.S. Bureau of Mines Minerals Yearbook 1937 and the League of Nations Statistical Year-Book 1937/38, rounded to each country's sum of spec §16.1 (USA 70, Germany 45, the UK 40, the USSR 25…). `noData` (KOR, PRK, TWN) fails any site, landmark, dam or downstream region on a region of those modern countries (spec §24-3, plan decision 33).
- `src/landmarks1936.json` names the region (`"TAG:name"`) of each of the thirteen landmarks (engine-api `LANDMARKS`, at most two a region: New York holds two) and of each of the nine dams (`DAMS`, one a region) with its downstream regions: at most three, each joined by a land edge to the dam's region or an earlier one of its list (the flood follows the river). Every id must be there once.
- `scenario.json` gives every region `coal`, `uranium` and `hydro` (0 when none), and `landmarks` and `dam` (`{id, downstream}`) where there are some. The tool prints every site's region and the sums per country.
